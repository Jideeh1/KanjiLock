# KanjiLock — /brag plan (v3, motion-graphics cut)

**Angle:** Fast, flashy motion-graphics edit. Every shot lands on the beat (128 BPM), with a new bold background color per shot.
**Hook:** A strobe of eight kanji (花 勉 食 学 本 語 日 鍵) on 8th notes, each on a new color, then "ONE WORD. / A DAY. / ON YOUR HOME SCREEN." slammed onto green.
**Techniques (from current motion-design practice):** kinetic type slams with overshoot and squash, elastic letter staggers, whip pans with directional motion blur, iris and diagonal slice wipes, zoom-throughs, camera shake and RGB split on hits, white flash on the drop, speed-ramped theme strobe, scrolling marquee bands, rotating radar rings and pulses, particle bursts on taps, ember particles, film grain.
