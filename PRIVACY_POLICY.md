# KanjiLock Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]

KanjiLock ("the app") is a Japanese vocabulary app. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The app has no user accounts and no servers of its own.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only in its private storage on your phone:

- words you accept, reject or add yourself ("mined" words)
- decks you import and your review history for them
- daily activity used for the streak calendar
- app settings

This data is not sent to the developer.

## Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to link Google Drive:

- The app asks for the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` permission. This only gives the app access to files it creates or that you open with it. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
- The app creates a folder named "KanjiLock" with one file, `kanjilock-data.json`, containing the data listed above.
- The file is transferred directly between your device and Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The developer never receives it.
- You can unlink Drive at any time in Settings. "Delete Drive copy" in Settings deletes the folder and file from your Drive. You can also remove the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

KanjiLock's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to provide the sync feature you turned on. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone else and not read by humans.

## AnkiDroid (optional)

If you connect AnkiDroid and grant the AnkiDroid database permission, the app reads deck names and cards from AnkiDroid on the same phone and sends your review answers back to AnkiDroid. This exchange stays on your device. AnkiDroid's own sync with AnkiWeb is governed by AnkiDroid's and AnkiWeb's policies.

## Other device features

- Text-to-speech uses the speech engine already installed on your phone.
- Notifications and reminders are created on your device.
- Files you export or share (CSV, Anki text, backups) go only where you choose to send them.

## What the app does not do

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no location, no contacts, no selling or sharing of data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The bundled dictionary is a general Japanese dictionary and contains adult vocabulary.

## Deleting your data

- Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to remove everything on your phone.
- Use "Delete Drive copy" in Settings, or delete the KanjiLock folder in Google Drive, to remove the synced copy.
