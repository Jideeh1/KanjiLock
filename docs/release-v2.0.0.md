## whats new

### practice mode and game modes
- practice mode is still a switch in the study tab, nothing in it touches your deck or anki
- turn it on and you get 4 game modes to pick from
- **reading** is the classic one, type the reading and it moves on
- **meaning** shows the word and you pick what it means out of 4
- **reverse** shows the meaning and you pick the word
- **listen** says the word out loud and you type what you heard, the kanji only shows after
- turn it off and youre back to normal reviews
- every mode works in focus mode too

### draw to search
- tap the pen in the dictionary search box and write a kanji with your finger
- tap the right guess and it goes into the search
- the handwriting model downloads from google the first time, after that it works offline and your writing stays on your phone

### achievements
- 49 achievements in 8 categories, daily streak, words, reviews, reading, meaning, reverse, listen and a special one
- the game modes have their own, how many you got right and your best run in a row
- when you unlock one a little trophy pill pops out at the top of the screen with its own sound, even in focus mode
- it grows out from the middle, the name and description drop in, then the trophy pops
- the day you got it gets a little star on the calendar

### stats
- new stats button at the top of the study tab
- reviews per day for the last 30 days, how you answer in each deck, and the insights card moved here
- all your achievements are in there too, one row per category showing what youre working on next, tap a category to see the rest

### export to anki
- export your kept words as a real `.apkg` deck
- opens in anki and ankidroid (then syncs to ankiweb like any deck), and importing it again updates the deck instead of making a copy

### looks
- 11 themes now, added ocean, ember, lavender, void and snow
- todays flame on the calendar and the streak counter are animated
- the nav bar is bigger and whats behind it blurs out with a soft feathered edge
- the tabs in your words fade at the edges and the selected one has a soft feathered glow
- widget text has a shadow so it stays readable on clear backgrounds

## changes

- accepted and rejected are now called **kept** and **skipped**
- the phone back button goes to the screen you were on before instead of closing the app
- add widget in settings and on the today screen now lets you pick any of the 4 widgets, not just the big one
- decks dont get a tag anymore, only ankiweb decks keep theirs
- the lock screen card is gone, widgets are home screen only now. if you had the lock screen notification on it clears itself after you update
- if the app ever crashes it saves the error and shows it the next time you open it, tap copy and send it to me

<br>

## good to know

- the blur behind the nav bar needs android 12 or newer, older phones get a soft fade instead
- draw to search needs internet once to get the model

<br>

## install

1. download `KanjiLock-2.0.0.apk` from the assets below
2. open it on your phone, it installs right over the last version and keeps everything
3. if youre already on 1.2.0 the app will ask you to update on its own

needs android 8.0 or newer
