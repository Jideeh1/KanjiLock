## whats in it

### daily word
- home screen widget with solid, semi and clear backgrounds
- lock screen widget on samsung (good lock) and android 16 qpr2+, notification for everyone else
- 3 buttons on the card. X rejects, gray rerolls, check keeps it
- words per day is up to you, default is 1

### study
- anki style reviews, again / hard / good / easy with little sounds
- type the reading in romaji and it turns into kana while you type
- import anki decks (`.apkg` / `.colpkg`) and kotoba decks (`.csv`)
- link ankidroid so your reviews go to ankiweb

### progress
- streak calendar where every day is a flame that fills up depending on how much you did
- insights for retention, mature cards and whats due this week
- study reminder and a streak saver at night

### dictionary
- around 218k words and 3k kanji, fully offline
- search in kanji, kana, romaji or english
- example sentences link back to tatoeba

### your data
- export your list as csv or for anki
- optional google drive sync, only touches its own file, delete it from settings anytime
- no accounts, no ads, no tracking

## install

1. download `KanjiLock-1.0.0.apk` from the assets below
2. open it on your phone
3. android will ask to allow installs from your browser or files app, thats normal for apps outside the play store

needs android 8.0 or newer

<br>

## heads up

> [!NOTE]
> google drive linking is still in testing, if it fails just skip it, the app works fine without it

- lock screen widgets depend on your phone, if yours doesnt support them the notification does the same job
- the dictionary is big so the first launch takes a sec to set it up
