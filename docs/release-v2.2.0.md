## whats new

### scan text
- the camera fills the whole screen now
- nothing gets read until you press the shutter button, no more freezing on the first thing it sees
- after the photo the text gets drawn right back on top of the photo where it was
- tap a word and a little card pops up with the reading and meaning, tap anywhere else and it goes away
- from the card you can hear it, add it to mined or open the full entry
- picking an image from your gallery works the same way

### calendar
- tap any day on the calendar to see what you did that day
- reviews, words kept, how many you got right, how you graded and any achievements you unlocked
- under the month it now says how many days you practiced and your best streak that month
- in deck, due and mined moved down to the review card so the calendar is shorter and the word of the day sits higher

### practice remembers where you left off
- every game keeps its own place, streak and score for each deck
- switch games or close the app and you pick up right where you were
- theres a reset button next to the other buttons if you want to start over, it asks first

### dictionary
- tap the box with the word to copy it
- the little badge on the corner shows its copyable and turns into a check when its copied

### settings
- themes are at the top now with a preview of the one youre on
- dark and light are split into tabs with an even grid so you dont have to scroll through all 26
- everything else is grouped, sound and feel, reminders, widgets, sync and backup, export, and clearing lists has its own box at the bottom

<br>

## fixes

- the meaning game doesnt show any kana in the choices anymore, it was giving the reading away
- reverse questions dont show kana either

## install

1. download `KanjiLock-2.2.0.apk` from the assets below
2. open it on your phone, it installs right over the last version and keeps everything
3. if youre already on 2.1.0 the app will ask you to update on its own

needs android 8.0 or newer
