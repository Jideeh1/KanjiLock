## whats new

### themes
- 6 themes in settings, teal, midnight, sakura, matcha, sumi and washi
- washi is the light one, the rest are dark
- tap one and the whole app switches right away, no restart

### widgets
- new dark widgets toggle in settings
- makes the background a lot darker so the text stays readable on light wallpapers
- works with solid, semi and clear

<br>

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jideeh1/KanjiLock/main/docs/screens-1.2.0.png" width="100%" alt="washi, sakura, midnight and matcha themes">

## fixes

- tapping show now scrolls down so the grade buttons are right there, no more scrolling to find them
- practice mode keeps the keyboard open when you get one right instead of closing and opening it again
- the deck tiles in the study tab update after you study, so a finished deck shows 0 new instead of your daily limit

<br>

## install

1. download `KanjiLock-1.2.0.apk` from the assets below
2. open it on your phone, it installs right over the last version and keeps everything
3. if youre already on 1.1.0 the app will ask you to update on its own

needs android 8.0 or newer
