## whats new

### study
- focus mode puts the card in the middle of a dark screen, buttons on top, typing underneath, and the keyboard stays open while you go
- practice mode, just type the reading and it jumps to the next one when you get it right. doesnt touch your reviews
- go back to the previous card and change your answer
- phone buzzes when what you type cant be right anymore, you can turn it off in settings

### widgets
- kanji tile, just todays kanji
- streak with the last 7 days of flames
- cards due with a study button
- the word widget has ‹ and › to go through todays words

### everything else
- swipe left and right on the today card to switch words
- the nav bar hides when you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up, the + only shows on your words
- your words has an accepted tab and a decks tab with the cards from your imported decks
- sync with ankiweb now walks you through it step by step, it goes through ankidroid since ankiweb only allows official anki apps
- the app checks github for new releases and asks before updating

download `KanjiLock-1.1.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. from now on the app tells you when theres a new version
