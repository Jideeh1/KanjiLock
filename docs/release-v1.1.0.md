## whats new

### focus mode
- the card sits in the middle of a dark screen and everything else gets out of the way
- grade buttons on top, kanji in the middle, typing underneath
- the keyboard stays open while you grade, tap outside the card to put it away
- enter shows the answer, enter again counts as good

### practice mode
- flip it on in the study tab, just type the reading and it jumps to the next card when you get it right
- doesnt touch your reviews or your anki scheduling
- show and skip if youre stuck, plus a counter for how many you got right in a row
- works in focus mode too

### study
- go back to the previous card and change your answer
- your phone buzzes the moment what you type cant be right anymore, turn it off in settings if you dont like it

### widgets
- kanji tile, just todays kanji in a 1x1
- streak, your streak with the last 7 days as flames
- cards due, how many are waiting with a study button
- the word widget now has ‹ and › to go through todays words

### your words
- the deck tab is now called accepted
- new decks tab that shows the cards from your imported anki and kotoba decks

### everything else
- swipe left and right on the today card to switch words
- the nav bar hides when you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up
- the + button only shows up on your words
- sync with ankiweb now walks you through it step by step through ankidroid
- the app checks for new releases when you open it and asks before updating, theres also a check for updates button in settings

## install

1. download `KanjiLock-1.1.0.apk` from the assets below
2. open it on your phone, it installs right over 1.0.0 and keeps your words and streak
3. android will ask to allow installs from your browser or files app, thats normal for apps outside the play store

from this version on the app lets you know when theres an update

needs android 8.0 or newer

<br>

## heads up

> [!NOTE]
> ankiweb only lets the official anki apps sign in, so syncing still goes through ankidroid

- android widgets cant do swipes, so the word widget uses the ‹ and › buttons instead
